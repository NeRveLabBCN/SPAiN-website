# SPAiN - Spanish Partnership for Autoimmune Neuropathies

This is the website for the **Spanish Partnership for Autoimmune Neuropathies (SPAiN)** project. The site provides a comprehensive protocol and resources for the coordination, collection, and management of clinical and biological data for patients with autoimmune neuropathies across Spain.

## About the Project

SPAiN aims to standardize and facilitate collaborative research on autoimmune neuropathies (NAi) by providing clear guidelines for:

- Patient selection and categories (adults, cancer-associated, pediatric)
- Informed consent procedures
- Data registration in REDCap
- Sample collection, processing, and labeling
- Sample shipment logistics
- Retrieval of research results

## Website Sections

The website is structured into the following main sections:

1. **Selección de Pacientes**  
   Criteria for including adult, cancer-associated, and pediatric patients with various autoimmune neuropathies.

2. **Consentimientos**  
   Information and downloadable forms for obtaining the required informed consents from participants.

3. **Registro**  
   Guidelines for registering patient data in the REDCap database, including de-identified coding and access instructions.

4. **Muestras**  
   Detailed instructions for collecting, processing, and prioritizing fundamental and optional biological samples (serum, DNA, PBMC, RNA, CSF) from patients.

5. **Rotulación**  
   Protocol for coding and labeling all samples, including timing and format examples.

6. **Envío**  
   Logistics for shipping samples (ambient or frozen), including contacts for the transport company and dry ice requests.

7. **Resultados**  
   Guidance for accessing patient-specific results via the NeuromusculasBCN portal.

## Technologies Used
